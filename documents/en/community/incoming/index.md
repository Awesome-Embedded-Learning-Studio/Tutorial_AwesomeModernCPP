# Community Contributions: First Issue

This section showcases community submissions that have passed basic checks and are ready for public reading and discussion.

Articles in this first issue are picked up by the online documentation site and may also be cited in the TAMCPP weekly newsletter. They are not final, settled versions of main tutorial content, and may continue to be revised based on reader feedback, discussions on external platforms, and maintainer review.

## Status Guidelines

Articles that enter this section usually satisfy the following:

- They render correctly.
- They have no obvious technical flaws.
- Their sources, references, images, and code are clearly documented.
- The author has agreed to public display and allows maintainers to make necessary edits.

## How to Contribute

See the [submission guide](../submit/) to send a Markdown draft or open a PR.

If you want to submit an article, you can open a PR and place the Markdown file in this directory. We recommend lowercase English filenames with hyphens, for example:

```text
documents/community/incoming/my-first-modern-cpp-note.md
```

You can submit just the article body first — no need to round out the full frontmatter, navigation, and indexing from the start. Maintainers will help tidy things up.

Provide the following when submitting; maintainers will place author credits in independent metadata:

- The title.
- The author, or how you would like to be credited.
- The target audience.
- Whether the content is original.
- The main reference materials.
