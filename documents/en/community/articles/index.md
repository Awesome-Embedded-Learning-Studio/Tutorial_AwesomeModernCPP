# Reviewed and Included

This section collects community articles that have been through discussion, revision, and maintainer review.

Compared with the First Issue section, articles here are closer to long-term citable material: the wording, terminology, code, cited sources, and applicable boundaries have been settled into a reasonably stable state. They keep their identity as community articles and do not necessarily belong to the main tutorial volumes.

## Inclusion Criteria

Before an article enters this section, it usually needs to satisfy the following:

- Technical conclusions have been reviewed by maintainers.
- Terminology, code blocks, references, and image sources have been organized.
- The article structure is suitable for long-term reading.
- It does not visibly conflict with existing content in the main tutorial.
- Author attribution, licensing, and citation boundaries are clear.

## Where Articles Go Next

Some articles may simply stay in the community articles section as supplementary reading material.

If an article fits the main tutorial particularly well, the maintainers can, with the author's understanding, give it a second round of editing and fold it into the corresponding volume, chapter, or topic.
