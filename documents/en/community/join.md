# Join the Conversation

We now have a reader chat group on QQ: stuck on a passage, want to push back on a chapter that reads badly (yes, some content had LLM help — we are working tirelessly to cut that down!), or just feel like talking C++ with someone — come on in.

<QQGroupCard />

## Which Channel for What

- **QQ group**: quick questions, casual chat, and nudges for the next installment. (Chinese-language chat.)
- **GitHub Discussions**: open-ended discussion — roadmap suggestions, learning notes, topics worth unpacking.
- **GitHub Issues**: concrete content proposals, submission topics, and bug reports.
- **Email**: <725610365@qq.com>, for things you would rather not discuss in public.

Valuable discussions are better kept in Discussions or Issues — they stay searchable instead of sinking into chat history.
