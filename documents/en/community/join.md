# Join the Conversation

We now have a reader chat group: stuck somewhere mid-read, want to gripe that a certain article goes round and round (some of the content did have LLM involvement — but we are working persistently to reduce it!), or just feel like finding someone to talk C++ with — everyone is welcome to join.

<QQGroupCard />

## Which Channel for What

- **QQ group**: quick questions, casual chat, and feedback urging the next installment — whatever pops into your head.
- **GitHub Discussions**: open-ended discussion — roadmap suggestions, learning notes, topics worth unpacking.
- **GitHub Issues**: concrete content proposals, submission topics, and bug reports.
- **Email**: <725610365@qq.com>, for things that are awkward to discuss in public.

Discussions with lasting value are best kept in Discussions or Issues — that is where they accumulate and stay searchable, instead of sinking into the chat log where no one can find them.
