# Embedded Development

This track is being rebuilt around STM32F407 and the Renode simulator, so a PC is all you need to follow along, no microcontroller required.

The new F407 + Renode line is currently Chinese-first; the English translation will follow once the Chinese draft stabilizes.

There's also a [STM32F103 line](f103/) for the F1 chip, maintained alongside the F407 line.
