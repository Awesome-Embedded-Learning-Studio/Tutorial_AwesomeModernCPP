# Linux Processes

This process chapter starts from the lifecycle of processes themselves: the first article walks the full road through the semantics of fork, the transformation of exec, the reaping of zombies, and the adoption of orphans, collecting everything into an RAII child_process wrapper. The series-wide common utilities (`unique_fd`, `sys_call`, `errno_code`) follow the definitions given in the two [Thinking Foundations](../../thinking/) articles; articles in this directory only reference them and do not redefine them.
